Section 1

{{ICON:compass}}What Evalu8 Compass is for

Evalu8 Compass helps leaders and staff answer one question honestly: how well are we doing?

It sets out what inspectors look for under Ofsted's renewed school inspection framework, matched to your school's type (maintained, academy or independent), your phases (EYFS to post-16) and any specialist provision. You rate each expectation, record your evidence, hear from every member of staff, and turn what you find into a plan you can act on.

{{ICON:users-group}} Built for co-creation

Self-evaluation is strongest when it is shared. Leaders rate the school, staff rate the same expectations anonymously, and the differences become the most useful conversations.

{{ICON:sparkles}} AI is your choice

Everything works without AI. If you switch it on, an AI "inspector" suggests improvement ideas and drafts plans. It only runs when you press an AI button, and it never changes your ratings.

{{ICON:alert-triangle}}

Advisory, not a judgement. Ratings are your own, and AI suggestions are advice. Nothing in Evalu8 Compass is an Ofsted judgement or predicts one. Always check current guidance: requirements change.

Section 2

{{ICON:lock}}Getting started: your access code

Your school is given one access code, in the form ABCD-EFGH-JKLM. It opens your school's checklist, action plan, progress, reports and workshops.

  1. Go to evalu8compass.app and choose Start the self-evaluation.
  2. Type your access code (capitals and dashes don't matter) and choose Enter.
  3. You stay signed in on that device for 30 days. Use Sign out in the top bar on a shared computer.

One copy for your whole team

Everything is saved to your school's account. Everyone who signs in with your code sees and edits the same copy, on any device. Two people can work at once: changes to different lines are kept, and colleagues' changes appear within about 20 seconds.

The badge at the top of the checklist shows whether your work is saved: Saved to your school's account, Saving…, or Offline: saved on this device. If the connection drops, keep working; changes are sent when it comes back.

{{ICON:lock}}

Treat the code like a password. Share it only with the leaders who need it. If it is passed on by mistake, email support@evalu8compass.app: we'll replace it straight away and your work is kept.

Staff never need the code. They join workshops with a separate 6-character workshop code.

Section 3 continued

Step 2: upload your latest Ofsted report

Straight after your school's details, upload your most recent Ofsted report. Choose Choose PDF (or paste the report's text), then Read the report. The AI reads it once and saves:

  • the inspection date, the framework, the safeguarding outcome and each area's grade;
  • the report's next steps, in order;
  • which checklist lines the report raised. These lines are marked Ofsted concern (or Ofsted strength) in the checklist.

You only do this once. The AI inspector's advice and readiness review take the report into account, it appears in your self-evaluation report, and every staff workshop you start offers to use it.

New independent schools

If your school is independent and hasn't yet had its first standard inspection, tick New school in Step 1 and enter the month you opened. Then:

  • In Step 2, upload your pre-registration inspection report. The AI reads which standards were judged likely or unlikely to be met, and flags anything unlikely to be met on your checklist.
  • A Ready for your first standard inspection? panel lists what must be up and running, in order: before pupils start, the first half-term, by the end of the first term, and before the inspection. Each item shows its status from your ratings, with the standard it comes from.
  • First inspection readiness gives an AI evaluation against each part of the independent school standards, four priorities, and the evidence of implementation to build up. Your first standard inspection comes within your first year of operation.
{{ICON:alert-triangle}}

The reading is done by AI. Check the grades and next steps against your report. Reading it uses one AI request and needs AI suggestions switched on in Step 1. No report yet, for example a new school? Skip this step.

Section 4

{{ICON:list-check}}Rate the checklist

Each evaluation area lists what inspectors look for. Rate every line and note your evidence.

RatingUse it when
MetYou can evidence it consistently, across the school.
PartlyIt's in place but not consistent, or not yet embedded.
Not yetIt's a gap.
N/AIt genuinely doesn't apply to your school.
{{ICON:bulb}}

Be honest, and evidence-led. "Partly" with a clear note is more useful than a generous "Met". Inspectors will test what you say.

Keep names out of notes. Describe what happens, not who: "some subject staff log concerns late", not a colleague's name. Never record information about individual pupils.

Useful tools

  • Not yet assessed and Gaps only filter the list.
  • Collapse all folds the areas so you can work one at a time.
  • Save / load copies your whole self-evaluation as text, as an extra backup.
Section 5

{{ICON:sparkles}}The AI inspector (optional)

Two kinds of help, both advisory. Use them only if your school has AI switched on.

Ideas for one line

Rate a line Partly or Not yet and choose Ask the AI inspector. You get 3–4 activities ordered from quickest to longest, each with a lead, a timescale and the evidence it will produce, plus the question an inspector is likely to ask. Choose Add to action plan on any activity you want to keep.

Inspector review of the whole checklist

Choose Inspector review for indicative grades for each area, four priorities for the coming term, strengths to showcase, and a separate list of compliance checks (policies or statutory items to fix now).

{{ICON:sparkles}}

Monthly allowance. Each school has a monthly allowance of AI requests, shown under the AI switch. If it runs out, you'll see built-in suggestions until it resets on the 1st of the month. Everything else keeps working.

Section 6

{{ICON:target}}Build your action plan

Open the Action plan tab. Every action has an owner, a deadline and what success looks like.

Three ways to add actions

  • Add an action to write your own.
  • Add actions for my gaps creates one for every line rated Partly or Not yet.
  • Draft actions with AI drafts a plan from your gaps for you to edit.

Keep it moving

  • Set each action to Not started, In progress or Done.
  • Overdue actions are flagged. Use the Overdue filter before a leadership meeting.
  • Link an action to a checklist line so reports show them together.
Section 7

{{ICON:history}}Termly snapshots and progress

Capture where you are at the end of each term, then show what has changed.

  1. Open the Progress tab. The snapshot name is suggested from the term (for example "Autumn 2026"); change it if you like.
  2. Choose Save snapshot. Your ratings and action plan are copied as they are today.
  3. Next term, choose Compare with now on that snapshot, or pick any two snapshots to compare.
{{ICON:bulb}}

Use Report with this comparison to add a "Progress since…" section to your report. It's strong evidence of improvement for governors and inspectors.

Section 8

{{ICON:file-export}}Reports: PDF and Word

Choose Report on the checklist tabs for a formatted self-evaluation report with your logo.

  • Tick what to include: lines rated Met, lines not yet assessed, evidence notes, the action plan, the AI review, and progress since a snapshot.
  • Save as PDF opens your browser's print window: choose Save as PDF as the destination.
  • Download Word (.docx) gives an editable document, ideal for governors' papers. It's also the most accessible version for screen-reader users.

Each workshop has its own report too (section 9).

Section 9

{{ICON:presentation}}Running a staff workshop

Staff rate the school anonymously on their own phones. You see the collated findings live, then turn them into an improvement plan.

Before: start the workshop

  1. Sign in with your access code, then open Staff workshop.
  2. Under Run a workshop, check your school's name, type and phases. They're filled in from your checklist.
  3. Under Latest Ofsted report, keep Use your saved report ticked, or choose a different PDF for this workshop.
  4. Choose the areas to cover. Two or three areas suit a 45–60 minute session; the page shows how many lines that is.
  5. Choose Start workshop.

Suggested 60-minute session

5 minWhy we're doing this. Answers are anonymous, there are no wrong answers, and no names in comments.
20 minStaff join and rate the lines on their phones.
15 minShare findings. Look at where staff agree and where views differ.
15 minDiscuss the splits and gaps; agree ratings together.
5 minWhat happens next, and when staff will see the plan.
Section 9 continued

During: staff join and rate

  • Share findings with staff sends the collated results to everyone's phone when you're ready to discuss them. Present mode enlarges the findings for the projector.
  • Close responses stops new answers when you've finished rating.
  • Copy facilitator link lets you reopen the workshop on another device. Keep this link private: it gives full control of the workshop.

Live findings

{{ICON:users}}

Anonymity is built in. No names or accounts. A role's breakdown (for example "Teaching assistants") is only shown when at least three people in that role have answered. Comments are shown without names.

Section 9 continued

After: agree ratings and generate the plan

  1. Agree ratings. On each line choose the agreed rating after discussion, and add a note. Agree most common answers fills any you haven't agreed with the staff's most common answer: check these before relying on them.
  2. Open the Improvement plan tab and complete the four steps:
    • About your setting: pupil numbers and needs, recent changes, current priorities. No names.
    • Latest Ofsted report: already filled in if you used your saved report. Otherwise Choose PDF (or paste the text), then Read the report.
    • Summarise staff comments groups comments into themes.
    • Generate plan.
Section 9 continued

The improvement plan

The plan has four priorities for the coming term, led by your Ofsted report's next steps and where staff and leaders agree. Each priority is tagged with the evidence behind it (ofsted, staff, agreed, context) and has 2–3 actions with a lead, a date and the evidence each will produce. Policy and statutory gaps are listed separately as compliance checks to fix now. You also get quick wins and a short message to share with staff.

Then

  • Share plan with staff shows the priorities and your message on staff phones.
  • Copy plan copies it as text for your school development plan.
  • On the Export tab, Send to our checklist adds the agreed ratings (with the staff split as an evidence note) and the plan's actions to your school's checklist and action plan.
  • The Workshop report gives participation, findings, where views differ, themes, the Ofsted reading and the plan, as PDF or Word.
Section 10 · copy or project this page for staff

{{ICON:users-group}}Joining a staff workshop

It takes about 20 minutes, and it's anonymous.

  1. On your phone, scan the QR code on the screen, or go to evalu8compass.app and type the 6-character code.
  2. Choose your role: teacher, teaching assistant, support staff, senior leader, governor/trustee or other.
  3. Choose Join. No name, email or account is needed.
  4. For each line choose Met Partly Not yet or Don't know, from your own experience.
  5. Add a comment if you like: what's working, what isn't, an example.
{{ICON:lock}}

Your answers are anonymous. Your role is only shown when at least three people with that role have answered.

Please don't name pupils or colleagues in comments. Describe what happens, not who.

Answers save as you go. You can change them until the facilitator closes responses. When findings or the plan are shared, they appear on your phone.

Section 11

{{ICON:shield-check}}Your data and privacy

  • Your school controls its information. Only people with your school's access code can see your self-evaluation, action plan and snapshots.
  • Workshops are anonymous. Staff give a role only. Exports and reports contain collated findings, never individual responses.
  • AI is optional and runs only when someone presses an AI button. The AI provider (Anthropic) doesn't train on your information and deletes it within 30 days.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking. Everything runs from our own server over an encrypted connection, with daily encrypted backups.
  • Retention: workshops are deleted 90 days after they were last used. Your school's saved work is kept while you have access, then deleted. Ask us at any time to delete it sooner.

For your data protection officer, the DPIA information for schools page at evalu8compass.app/dpia.html sets out how the service works, the risks and how they're reduced. See also the privacy notice, terms and accessibility statement, linked at the foot of every page.

{{ICON:alert-triangle}}

On a shared computer, sign out when you've finished. A copy of your work is kept in that browser for offline use until you clear the site's data.
