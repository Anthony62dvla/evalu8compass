1. About these terms

Evalu8 Compass is provided by {{OPERATOR}} ("we", "us"). These terms apply to the school, academy trust or other organisation given an access code ("your school"), and to everyone who uses the service through it. By using the service, your school agrees to these terms. Our privacy notice explains how we handle personal information.

Evalu8 Compass is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Ofsted or the Department for Education.

2. Access codes

  • Schools sign up on our sign-up page. We email the access code to the address given, so use an address the school controls. If the code is lost, we'll email a new one to the same address.
  • Your school's access code opens its self-evaluation, action plan, snapshots and workshops. Treat it like a password: share it only with the leaders who need it.
  • Your school is responsible for who it gives the code to and for what's done using it.
  • If you think the code has been shared with someone who shouldn't have it, tell us at support@evalu8compass.app. We'll revoke it and issue a new one. Revoking a code doesn't delete your work.
  • Staff joining a workshop use a separate, temporary workshop code and don't need your school's access code.

3. Using the service properly

Your school agrees not to:

  • enter personal information about individual pupils, or special category data (for example health, SEND details about a named child, or ethnicity), in notes, comments, actions or uploads;
  • upload documents other than your school's published Ofsted report and the context you choose to share;
  • try to get into another school's information, test or bypass our security, or overload the service;
  • use the service, or its AI features, in a way that breaks the law.

We may suspend access if the service is being misused, and will tell your school why.

4. AI, criteria and professional judgement

  • The checklist reflects our reading of Ofsted's school inspection framework, the independent school standards and DfE statutory guidance. Requirements change, so check current guidance before an inspection.
  • AI suggestions, indicative grades and plans are advisory. They are not Ofsted judgements and don't predict inspection outcomes.
  • Decisions about your school, including what to record in its self-evaluation and improvement plan, remain your school's professional responsibility.

5. Your school's content

Your school owns what it records in Evalu8 Compass. It gives us permission to store and process that content only to provide the service to your school. Your school can download its self-evaluation and workshop reports as PDF or Word documents at any time.

When we process personal information in your school's content, your school is the controller and we are its processor. These terms are the contract required by Article 28 of the UK GDPR. We will:

  1. process it only to provide the service, as your school instructs through these terms and its use of the service, unless the law requires otherwise (in which case we'll tell your school unless the law forbids it);
  2. make sure anyone who can access it is bound by confidentiality;
  3. keep it secure using the measures described in our DPIA information;
  4. only use the sub-processors listed in our privacy notice, give your school at least 30 days' notice of any change so it can object, and hold each sub-processor to data protection terms at least as protective as these;
  5. help your school respond to requests from people exercising their data protection rights, and with DPIAs and consultation with the ICO;
  6. tell your school without undue delay, and within 48 hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting its information, and help it meet its own obligations;
  7. delete your school's information when it asks, or automatically {{RETENTION_DAYS}} days after its access ends (workshops are deleted 90 days after they were last used), except where the law requires us to keep it. Encrypted backups expire within 90 days;

Details of the processing (subject matter, duration, nature, purpose, types of data and people concerned) are set out in our DPIA information for schools.

We work to keep the service available and your school's information safe, including daily encrypted backups. We may need to pause the service briefly for maintenance or updates, and can't promise it will always be available or free of errors. For help, email support@evalu8compass.app.

8. Free term, prices and payment

Free first term

Your school can use everything free from the day it signs up until the end of that school term. If it signs up after that term's half-term, the next term is free as well. We use typical English school terms: the autumn term ends on 31 December, the spring term ends when the summer term starts after the Easter holidays, and the summer term ends on 31 August. The exact last day is in the email with your access code and on your dashboard. Nothing is charged for the free term and there's nothing to cancel. If you haven't subscribed by then, access continues for 14 more days so you have time to arrange it.

Subscribing and paying

  • Someone authorised to commit the school subscribes from the dashboard or our subscribe page. The paid year lasts 12 months and starts when the free term ends (including if you subscribe during the 14 days after it), or on the day you subscribe if access has already paused.
  • Your access continues from the moment you subscribe. Your dashboard shows your subscription as paid once we've received payment.
  • We email an invoice when you subscribe. It's payable by bank transfer within 30 days of the invoice date, or by the start of the paid year if that's later. Give a purchase order number when you subscribe if your school needs one; the terms printed on a purchase order don't apply unless we agree to them in writing.
  • If an invoice is unpaid when it's due, we'll send a reminder. If it's still unpaid 30 days after it was due, we may pause access until it's paid. Your saved work is kept.

Extra AI requests

  • Each subscription includes a monthly allowance of AI requests, shown next to the AI switch. When 10 or fewer are left, your school can order extra requests from the checklist page.
  • We add extra requests once we've approved the order, and email an invoice payable within 30 days. Extra requests are used only after the monthly allowance has run out, and don't expire at the end of the month. They end when your school's access ends.
  • Extra requests can't be refunded once used. If we decline an order, nothing is charged.

Renewing, cancelling and refunds

  • Subscriptions don't renew automatically. We'll email you 30 days before the paid year ends, and your dashboard shows a reminder. If you haven't renewed by the end of the paid year, access continues for 30 more days while you renew. The new year starts on the day the previous one ended, so those 30 days count towards it. If you don't renew within the 30 days, access pauses.
  • You can cancel up to 30 days after a paid year starts (or at any time before it starts), and we'll cancel the invoice or refund it in full. After that, there are no refunds for the rest of the year, and access continues until the year ends.
  • If we stop providing the service, or end your school's access for any reason other than misuse, we'll refund the unused full months of the paid year.
  • We won't change your price during a paid year. We'll tell you about any price change at least 60 days before your renewal date.

When access pauses

When the 14 days after a free term, or the 30 days after a paid year, end without a subscription, your school's access code stops working, but its saved work is kept. Subscribing makes the same code work again straight away. If your school doesn't subscribe, its information is deleted {{RETENTION_DAYS}} days after access ended, as set out in section 6.

9. The consultant register

  • Signed-in schools can search our register of consultants and school improvement partners and send them enquiries. Before listing anyone, we check their identity, their enhanced DBS certificate's status on the DBS Update Service, the Teaching Regulation Agency's prohibition records, their insurance and two references, and show the date of each check.
  • Those checks don't replace your school's own responsibilities. Before a consultant works in your school, carry out your own checks as Keeping children safe in education requires.
  • Any work is agreed directly between your school and the consultant. We're not a party to it, don't employ or supply consultants, and aren't responsible for the quality of their work. Tell us at support@evalu8compass.app about any concern.
  • When you send an enquiry, your name, role, email address, any phone number and your message are sent to the consultant. Don't include pupils' personal information.

10. Responsibility for loss

Nothing in these terms limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, or for anything else that can't be limited by law. Otherwise, we aren't responsible for inspection outcomes or for decisions your school makes using the service, or for indirect or unforeseeable loss.

11. Ending access

Your school can stop using the service at any time and can ask us to delete its information. Section 8 explains cancelling a subscription. We'll give reasonable notice before ending a school's access, except where it's needed to deal with misuse or a security risk. After access ends, information is deleted as set out in section 6.
