Why it exists

Our intention

Inspection should never be a surprise. Evalu8 Compass gives schools a calm, structured way to look at themselves the way an inspector would, well before anyone arrives, so energy goes into improving rather than performing.

An honest picture

117 expectations drawn from the renewed framework, the independent school standards and DfE guidance, matched to your type of school and the phases you teach. Each line asks for evidence, not opinion.

Every voice counts

Teachers, teaching assistants, support staff, leaders and governors rate the school anonymously. Where views differ, you find out where practice isn't yet consistent: the conversations worth having.

From findings to action

Gaps become priorities with owners, timescales and the evidence each one will produce. Optional AI suggestions help you draft; your professional judgement decides.

Co-creation

What we ask of you

Evalu8 Compass works best as something you build with your staff, and something we keep building with schools.

In your school

  • Be honest, not hopeful. "Partly" and "Not yet" are useful answers. A self-evaluation that's all green helps nobody.
  • Bring evidence. Note what you'd show an inspector for each line. If you can't find it, that's a finding too.
  • Involve everyone. Run a workshop so every role is heard, not only senior leaders.
  • Protect anonymity. Never try to work out who said what. Role breakdowns only appear for groups of three or more.
  • Keep people out of comments. Describe what happens, not who. Never name pupils or colleagues.
  • Lead with judgement. AI suggestions are a starting point. Leaders decide what goes in the plan.

With us

  • This is an early version. It will change as schools use it, and some features below are still being built.
  • Tell us what's missing. A line that doesn't fit your setting, a report you need, wording that confuses staff: your feedback shapes the next version. Email support@evalu8compass.app.
  • Check what matters. Statutory guidance changes, especially each September. Treat references as a guide and check the current version.
  • We're not Ofsted. Nothing here is an inspection judgement or predicts one.

How to use it

Six steps, at your own pace

  1. Set up your school

    Open the checklist, add your school's name and logo, choose your school type and the phases you teach. The checklist adjusts to fit.

  2. Rate each expectation

    Mark each line Met, Partly, Not yet or N/A and add your evidence. Your progress and area scores update as you go.

  3. Bring your staff in

    Start a staff workshop, pick two or three areas and share the code. Staff answer on their phones in 20–30 minutes.

  4. Share and agree

    Show staff the collated findings, discuss where views differ, and record the school's agreed rating for each line.

  5. Plan improvements

    Turn gaps into actions with an owner, a deadline and what success looks like. Ask the AI inspector for suggestions only if you want them.

  6. Keep the record

    Download your evaluation report as a PDF or Word document, track your action plan, and repeat each term to show how far you've come.

AI is your choice

Everything works without AI. Switch AI suggestions off in your school settings and every AI button disappears. When it's on, AI only runs when you press an AI button, and it never changes your ratings or adds actions you haven't accepted.

  • Its suggestions are advisory and are never an Ofsted judgement.
  • When you use it, the relevant lines and notes are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to produce the answer.
  • Keep names and personal details out of notes and comments.

Your data

  • Checklist and action plan: saved to your school's account on our server, so your leadership team shares one copy. Only people with your school's access code can see it.
  • Workshops: staff join with a code and a role only, with no names or accounts. Workshop records are deleted 90 days after they were last used.
  • Exports contain collated findings only, never individual responses.
