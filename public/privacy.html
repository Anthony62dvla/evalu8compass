Privacy notice

How Evalu8 Compass handles information about the schools, leaders and staff who use it.

Last updated {{UPDATED}}

In short

  • We collect as little personal information as we can. Staff join workshops with a role only: no names, no accounts.
  • Your school's self-evaluation and action plan are saved on our server. Only people with your school's access code can open them.
  • We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and we never sell or share your information for marketing.
  • AI is optional. When you use it, the relevant text is sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, to produce a suggestion. Anthropic doesn't train its models on it and deletes it within 30 days.
  • Your school can ask us to delete its information at any time.

2. Our role and your school's role

Your school is in charge of its own self-evaluation and workshops. Under UK data protection law, your school is the controller of what it records in Evalu8 Compass (ratings, evidence notes, action plans, snapshots and workshop content). We act as its processor: we only use that information to provide the service to your school, following the data processing terms in our terms of use.

We are the controller for a small amount of information we need to run the service ourselves: the contact details given when a school signs up, invoicing details, emails sent to support, and security records.

Schools completing a data protection impact assessment (DPIA) can use our DPIA information for schools.

5. What we don't do

  • We don't ask for or need pupils' personal information, or any special category data such as health or ethnicity.
  • We don't use advertising, analytics, tracking pixels or third-party scripts. Fonts and icons are served from our own server.
  • We don't sell information or share it for marketing.
  • We don't use your school's information to train AI models, and neither does our AI provider.
  • We don't look at a school's self-evaluation unless the school asks us to help with something. Our admin console shows only counts (for example "42 lines rated"), never content.

6. AI suggestions

AI suggestions are optional. Your school can switch them off in its settings, and then nothing is sent to the AI. When they're on, AI only runs when someone presses an AI button.

When you use an AI feature, we send our AI provider only what it needs for that request: for example the checklist line, your rating and evidence note, and your school's type and phases. For workshop features, it's the collated findings, anonymous comments, your context and the Ofsted report text. We don't send access codes or anything that identifies who is using the tool.

Our AI provider is Anthropic PBC, based in the United States. Under its commercial terms, Anthropic does not train its models on information sent through its API, and it deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days (unless it must keep them longer to enforce its usage policy or meet a legal requirement). Because this involves sending information to the US, the transfer relies on the safeguards in Anthropic's data processing terms. You can read Anthropic's retention policy and privacy policy.

AI suggestions are advisory. They are not an Ofsted judgement and don't predict one.

7. Where information is stored

The service runs on a server in {{HOSTING}}. Everything is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

We back up the service every day. Backups are encrypted (AES-256) on our server before they're copied anywhere, and are then kept in {{BACKUP}}. Local backups are kept for 14 days and off-site backups for 90 days, so information you delete can remain in an encrypted backup for up to 90 days before it's gone completely.

A copy of your school's self-evaluation is also kept in the browser of each device that opens it, so work isn't lost if the connection drops. Signing out doesn't remove it: clear your browser's site data for evalu8compass.app on a shared computer.

9. Keeping it secure

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS) for everything, with strict browser security settings.
  • Access codes are stored only as hashes. Sign-ins use signed cookies that can't be read by scripts, and a revoked code stops working straight away.
  • Each school's information is stored separately and is only reachable with that school's code.
  • Repeated wrong guesses at a code are blocked.
  • The superadmin console needs a password and an authenticator app code. Every admin action is logged.
  • The app runs with the minimum permissions it needs, and daily backups are encrypted.

If something goes wrong that affects your school's information, we'll tell your school without undue delay.

11. Your rights

You have rights to ask for a copy of your personal information, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to how it's used, and to have it in a portable format.

  • For your school's self-evaluation or workshops, contact your school first, because it decides how that information is used. We'll help your school respond.
  • For anything else, email support@evalu8compass.app. We'll reply within one month.

Workshop responses are anonymous, so we usually can't link a response to a particular person.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or 0303 123 1113. We'd appreciate the chance to put things right first.
