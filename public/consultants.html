Find a consultant or SIP

Search for a checked consultant or school improvement partner by where you are and what you need help with. Send an enquiry and they'll reply to you directly.

Every consultant here has had their identity, enhanced DBS status on the Update Service, the teacher prohibition list, insurance and two references checked by Evalu8 Compass, on the dates shown. Before they work in your school, you must still carry out your own checks as Keeping children safe in education requires, for example seeing their DBS certificate and checking its status yourself. Any work is agreed between your school and the consultant.
