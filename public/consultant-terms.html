1. About the register

The register is provided by {{OPERATOR}} ("we", "us") as part of Evalu8 Compass. It lets schools signed in to Evalu8 Compass find consultants and school improvement partners ("you") by location and expertise, and send you enquiries.

  • No guarantee of work. Being listed, and passing our checks, doesn't guarantee that any school will contact you or engage you, or that you'll receive any number of enquiries. Schools decide for themselves whether to contact you and whether to work with you.
  • We are not your employer. Listing on the register doesn't create employment, worker, agency, partnership or any other relationship between you and us beyond these terms. We don't pay you, direct your work or supply you to schools. You work for yourself (or through your own company) and are responsible for your own tax, National Insurance, insurance and business arrangements.
  • We are not an agency. We don't find work for you, recommend you to particular schools, negotiate on your behalf, or take a commission. Any work is agreed directly between you and the school.

2. Checks

  • To be listed you need an enhanced DBS certificate (child workforce) registered on the DBS Update Service. You agree to show us the certificate and consent to us checking its status when you join and every year after.
  • We also check your identity, the Teaching Regulation Agency's records of teachers prohibited from teaching, your professional indemnity and public liability insurance, and two professional references.
  • We record only the date and outcome of each check. We don't keep copies of your DBS certificate or details of what it contains.
  • You must tell us straight away, by emailing support@evalu8compass.app, if your DBS status changes, your insurance ends, you become subject to a prohibition or restriction, or anything else happens that a school would reasonably need to know.
  • Our checks don't replace a school's own duties. Schools are told to carry out their own checks under Keeping children safe in education before you work with them, and you agree to help them do so.

3. Your listing

  • Everything in your listing must be accurate and up to date. Describe your experience honestly and don't overstate qualifications or roles.
  • Don't use Ofsted's name or logo, or a current role as an inspector, to promote your services, and don't name schools, pupils or staff without permission.
  • Your listing shows your town, postcode district, region and how far you travel. It never shows your address, email or phone number.
  • Once your listing is approved, changes to what schools see are checked by us before they're published. We may edit or decline changes that don't meet these terms.

4. Fees

  • Listing fee: £49, one-off. We invoice it when your checks are complete and your listing is approved. There's nothing to pay if your application isn't approved. Your listing goes live once the fee is paid.
  • Annual compliance check: £18 a year. We invoice it a month before each anniversary of your listing going live, and repeat the DBS status and insurance checks.
  • {{VAT_LINE}} Invoices are payable by bank transfer within 30 days.
  • Fees aren't refundable once your listing has gone live, including if you leave the register or your listing is hidden or suspended. If we close the register, we'll refund the annual check fee for any unused full months.
  • We don't charge schools to contact you, and we don't take a commission on work you agree with schools.

5. Working with schools

  • Reply to enquiries within five working days, even if you can't help.
  • Your contract, fees and arrangements with a school are between you and the school. We aren't a party to them and aren't responsible for them.
  • Follow the school's safeguarding procedures, and handle any information about pupils, staff or the school lawfully and confidentially.

6. Hiding, suspending and removing listings

  • Your listing is hidden automatically if your insurance on our records ends, or if your annual check is more than 30 days overdue. It reappears when the check is complete.
  • We may suspend or remove a listing, and will tell you why, if information is inaccurate, a check raises a concern, schools report serious problems, or these terms are broken. Where there's a safeguarding concern, we may also need to share information with the relevant authorities.
  • You can remove your listing at any time from your private listing page. Your details are deleted straight away; invoices are kept for our accounts.

7. Your information

We're the controller of the information you give us for the register. Our privacy notice explains what we collect, why and for how long, including how we handle information about DBS checks.

8. General

Nothing in these terms limits liability that can't be limited by law. Otherwise, we aren't responsible for work you do or don't get through the register, or for indirect or unforeseeable loss. We may update these terms and will email you about important changes before they take effect. These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have jurisdiction.
