For consultants and school improvement partners

Join the Evalu8 Compass consultant register

Schools use Evalu8 Compass to evaluate themselves against Ofsted's framework and the independent school standards, then plan their next steps. When a school wants expert help with its priorities, it can search our register of checked consultants and SIPs by location and expertise, and contact you directly.

What schools see

  • Your name, one-line summary, bio, background and qualifications
  • Your areas of expertise, phases and types of school
  • Your town, region, how far you travel, and whether you work remotely. Never your address.
  • The checks we've made, with the month of each

Only schools signed in to Evalu8 Compass can see the register. Enquiries are emailed to you, and you reply to the school directly. Any work is agreed between you and the school.

What you need

  • An enhanced DBS certificate (child workforce) registered on the DBS Update Service, so we can check its status with your consent
  • Not barred from teaching: we check the Teaching Regulation Agency's records
  • Professional indemnity and public liability insurance
  • Two professional referees, for example a headteacher or trust leader you've worked with
  • Proof of identity

Fees

£49One-off listing fee, invoiced once your checks are complete and your listing is approved. Nothing to pay if it isn't.
£18 a yearAnnual compliance check: we recheck your DBS status and insurance each year to keep your listing current.

No VAT is charged at present. Fees are paid by bank transfer within 30 days of the invoice.

How it works

  1. Apply online. It takes about 10 minutes.
  2. We contact you to see your DBS certificate and check it on the Update Service, then check your identity, the teacher prohibition list, insurance and references.
  3. When everything is in place, we approve your listing and email the £49 invoice. Your listing goes live once it's paid.
  4. Each year we repeat the DBS and insurance checks for £18. If your insurance lapses or the annual check is more than 30 days late, your listing is hidden until it's done.
